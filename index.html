LabelLoop Label a little. Learn a lot. Nothing leaves your device.

What is this?

Small teams collect piles of open-text feedback - survey comments, program reviews, suggestion boxes - and then read every single one by hand, or pay for a cloud service that takes the data away. LabelLoop is a third option: active learning in your browser. You label a few comments, a small model trains on your device, and it shows you the comments it is most unsure about next - often the ones worth a look first. Suggestions focus your review; they are not a guarantee.

Everything runs locally in this tab: the text, the training, the model. There is no server. Save your work as a file and resume anywhere.

      Does active learning actually help?

      We simulated the labeling loop on the demo dataset (360 synthetic comments): start with 2 labels per theme, then repeatedly ask for 5 more - picked either by the model (active) or at random - retrain, and measure macro-F1 on a held-out 30% split the simulated labeler never sees. Three seeds; the band is ±1 standard deviation. The numbers below are computed by this repository's code (run_benchmark.mjs), not written by hand.

      Held-out quality with all labels

      These results come from a synthetic demo dataset with clean themes. Real feedback is messier and scores will be lower. Treat this as evidence that the labeling loop works end to end - not as a promised accuracy for your data.
